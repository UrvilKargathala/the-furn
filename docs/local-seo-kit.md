## 3. Asking for Google reviews

Reviews are the biggest local ranking factor you control, and AI assistants read them too.

1. In your Google profile, click **Ask for reviews** and copy the short link (it looks like `g.page/r/...`).
2. Send it personally (WhatsApp works best) to happy clients, starting with the five whose testimonials are on the site: Mrs. Rupal Shroff, Mrs. Trusha Patel, Poonam Patel, Mr. Deepak Shah and the Patel family.
3. Reply to every review within a few days, good or bad.

**Message you can send (edit freely):**

> Hi [Name], thank you again for trusting The Furn with your [kitchen/home]. Your kind words mean a lot to our team. Would you mind sharing them on Google too? It takes a minute and really helps other families in Surat find us: [review link]. Thank you! — Team The Furn

Rules: never offer discounts or gifts for reviews, never write reviews for clients, and don't filter who gets asked (Google calls asking only happy clients "review gating"). Over time, ask every finished client. Google removes reviews that break these rules.
