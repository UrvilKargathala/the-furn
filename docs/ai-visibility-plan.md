# AI visibility plan (GEO): The Furn

Goal: when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot or Google's AI Overview about modular kitchens or interiors in Surat, The Furn is named and described correctly.

## Where we stand (checked 26 Nov 2026)

**On our own site (done):**
- AI crawlers are allowed in robots.txt.
- `/llms.txt` is a short index and `/llms-full.txt` holds the whole site in one file.
- Structured data covers the business (with legal name, exact map pin and client reviews), services, FAQs, projects, breadcrumbs and page types.
- Every service page answers the common questions directly.

## Actions, in order

1. **Google reviews first** (local-seo-kit.md §3). Lists like Three Best Rated and every AI assistant weigh them.
2. **Directories with identical details** (local-seo-kit.md §1): Justdial, IndiaMART, Houzz, Sulekha, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
3. **Houzz portfolio:** upload 5–8 of the strongest projects from /works with the same names and descriptions, each linking back to its page.
4. **Editorial features:** pitch 2–3 finished projects with good photos to The Architects Diary, Re-Thinking The Future and Gujarati/Surat home or real-estate blogs. One feature that names "The Furn, Surat" and links to the site is worth more than many directory entries.
5. **Hardware brand partners:** ask Hettich, Blum, Häfele and others whether they list partner or dealer studios. A mention on a brand site is a strong trust signal.
6. **Builders and architects you work with:** ask them to credit "Interiors by The Furn" with a link on their project pages and social posts.
7. **Instagram, Pinterest and LinkedIn:** keep the name "The Furn", the website and the phone identical, and use project names that match the website.

## What to expect

AI answers change slowly and are hard to predict. Expect directory and review work to show up in 1–3 months. Brand questions (13–16) should improve first, because they depend mostly on our own site, which is now well structured. Discovery questions (1–7) depend on reviews and third-party mentions, so they take longest. Nobody can guarantee a mention or a ranking. Treat the monthly table as the measure, not a single answer on a single day.
